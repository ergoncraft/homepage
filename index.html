The ErgonCraft Manifesto

Software for the human mind, not for algorithms.

At ErgonCraft, we build tools that serve what you do in real life — then step out of the way.

  • Humans over Mechanisms

    We build tools that adapt to your life — at work, in engineering, and with family. Never the other way around.

  • Ergonomics of the Mind

    No slot-machine tricks, no doomscrolling, no disguised ad traps. Just functional clarity and conscious focus.

  • Transparent Ethics

    A fair price, zero data collection, and absolute respect for your privacy.

Technology is our future. But real life happens outside the screen.

ἔργον

The name ErgonCraft

Derived from the Ancient Greek word Ergon (ἔργον), meaning action, work, or deed — and the root of ergonomics, the science of designing tools for human use. Combined with Craft, it represents our core philosophy: creating precise, honest tools that serve human action in real life, rather than trapping the mind in the digital world.

Ergon Launcher

A calm home screen built on a gesture, not a grid. Hold, slide, release — and you are where you meant to go, before the screen can pull you anywhere else.

  • Press, slide, release

    Hold Start and your categories fan out. Slide onto one, release on an app. Muscle memory instead of hunting through icons.

  • Right under your thumb

    Start sits where your thumb rests, with a Favorites hub just above it. Both can be moved anywhere.

  • Profiles for your life

    Work, family, focus: each with its own apps, colours and call behaviour, switching automatically on your schedule.

  • Calm on demand

    Silent mode lets only your VIP contacts through. Optional grayscale takes the shine off the screen.

  • Declutter

    See which apps you haven't opened in months and remove them, one at a time.

  • Make it yours

    Calm built-in themes and four looks (circles, leaves, pie or ensō). You can even brush your own ensō.

More tools are on the workbench. They'll appear here when they're ready.
